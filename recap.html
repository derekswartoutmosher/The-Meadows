The Inaugural Invitational

Saturday · October 17, 2026 · Ann Arbor

It was a beautiful fall Saturday in October. A light frost on the ground when the day began, and a crisp football air to set the stage for the first-ever Meadows Invitational.

Nine players. One round of stroke play to determine seeds, then an eight-player match play event. The golf was played on six holes, with a par of 22. The signature pine anchored us, and played defense at the perfect times.

The people brought the vibes. We lived, laughed, and loved through the ups and downs of competition, from the football, to the course, to the fire.
